    Reading this dashboard

    Signal cards (right panel)

    Card size & color
    Conviction. Bigger, greener cards = higher measured win probability. Gray = below coin-flip odds.
    Win probability ring
    Chance this signal ends profitable, learned from every past signal of the same strategy (n = how many). "model" = the machine-learning estimate for these exact market conditions; "history" = the strategy's overall record.
    Entry ladder E1·E2·E3
    Scale-in plan: first entry at the signal, two more orders at better prices. A filled dot = that level traded. Your average entry updates as rungs fill.
    Trade gauge
    Where price sits right now between the stop (left) and profit territory (right). The dot is live price, in R units (1R = distance from entry to stop).
    BE / TRAIL chips
    Automatic management: at +1R a third of the position is banked and the stop moves to break-even (BE). At +1.5R the stop trails 1R behind the best price (TRAIL).
    banked
    Profit already secured by the +1R partial, in R.
    live R
    Total open result right now, in R units.
    Time bar
    How long the trade has been open, out of the 2-hour maximum.

    Market table (left)

    vol z
    How unusual current trading volume is vs the last 30 minutes. 0 = normal, +2.5 = strong burst, the level rules look for.
    flow (CVD)
    Buying minus selling pressure over 5 minutes. Rising line = buyers in control.
    depth
    Share of resting orders sitting on the bid. Above 0.65 = heavy buy-side wall.
    spread
    Cost to cross the book, in basis points. Tight spread = cheap to trade.
    funding
    Hourly rate longs pay shorts (positive) or shorts pay longs (negative). Bold = 7-day extreme, a crowding signal.
    ΔOI
    Change in open interest: rising = new positions entering.
    long / short score
    How many bullish/bearish rules agree right now, 0–100. 60+ = watch, 80+ = alert.
    warm · stale · qtn
    warm: statistics still warming up (~30 min after start, no signals yet). stale: no trades for 30s. qtn: data failed a self-audit check — signals suspended for that market.

    Completed list

    WIN / LOSS ± R
    Final result in R. Exit reason: TARGET-less system — STOP (initial stop hit), BE (break-even stop after banking), TRAIL (trailing stop, usually a win), TIME (2-hour limit).

    System reasoning (live decision log)

    What it is
    The system narrating its own decisions in real time, with the actual numbers: why each signal fired (and why one was held back), how every stop and entry ladder was constructed, each break-even / trailing-stop move, and exactly what it learned from every finished trade. This is how you verify the logic instead of trusting it blindly.
    signal
    Rule logic — the evidence behind a fire, the score math (weight × learned multiplier × strength), tier grading, cooldown suppressions.
    trade
    Trade management — ladder fills with the improving average entry, +1R banking, break-even and trailing decisions, exits with the full R accounting.
    learning
    Self-improvement — how the finished trade moved the strategy's measured record, its score weight, and the ML model's prediction for the same setup.
    audit
    Self-verification — periodic proof that data and logic checks pass, plus quarantine decisions when they don't.

    Bottom strip

    win rate / avg R / w× / ml
    Per strategy: measured win %, average R per signal (expectancy), the learned weight multiplier applied to its score, and how many samples the ML model has trained on.
