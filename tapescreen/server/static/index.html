    Reading this dashboard

    Session Performance

    net R / win rate / avg R
    Results of the simulated trade plans over the last 24h, in R units (1R = distance from entry to stop). The curve is cumulative R as trades resolve. Simulated plans, not orders — this tool never trades.

    Signal cards

    Card size & color
    Conviction. Bigger, greener cards = higher measured win probability. Gray = below coin-flip odds.
    Win probability ring
    Chance this signal ends profitable, learned from every past signal of the same strategy. "model" = the ML estimate for these exact conditions; "history" = the strategy's record.
    Entry ladder E1·E2·E3
    Scale-in plan: first entry at the signal, adds at better prices. Filled dot = that level traded.
    Trade gauge
    Live price between the stop (left) and profit territory (right), in R units.
    BE / TRAIL chips
    At +1R a third is banked and the stop moves to break-even. From +1.5R the stop trails 1R behind the best price.
    explore chip
    Opened through the probation exploration quota: the strategy is gated, but the desk keeps a controlled trickle of trades so it can earn its way back.

    Quant Desk & Tracked Variables

    Quant Desk
    A deterministic panel that reviews every resolved outcome: a performance analyst (expectancy per strategy), a risk officer (weak strategies go on probation behind a higher confidence bar), a feature scout (see below) and a loop-breaker that guarantees exploration so gating can never starve a strategy of data.
    Tracked Variables
    The model starts with 12 base inputs. The scout measures candidate variables (time of day, funding percentile, squeeze regime, OI thrust…) against every outcome; when one shows real predictive correlation it is PROMOTED into the models — the system discovers its own new data points. Variables whose evidence decays are retired.

    Validation Tracker

    The bar
    A strategy is VALIDATED for real-trading consideration only when its record clears all four gates: ≥150 resolved R-trades, ≥28 distinct active days (so the sample spans market regimes), average R confidently above zero at 95% (net of spread + taker fees), and off desk probation. A confidently negative record is REJECTED — a verdict that saves real money. Until a strategy is validated, the banner says plainly: do not trade real money on it.
    projected ready
    Estimated date the gates are met at the current trade rate. This only advances while the app runs — keep it running 24/7.

    Markets table

    vol z
    How unusual current volume is vs the last 30 minutes. 0 = normal, +2.5 = strong burst.
    flow (CVD)
    Buying minus selling pressure over 5 minutes.
    depth
    Share of resting orders on the bid. Above 0.65 = heavy buy-side wall.
    funding
    Hourly rate longs pay shorts (+) or shorts pay longs (−). Bold = 7-day extreme.
    long / short score
    Rule confluence 0–100. 60+ = watch, 80+ = alert.
    warm · stale · qtn
    warm: statistics warming (~30 min). stale: no trades 30s. qtn: failed a self-audit — signals suspended.

    System Reasoning

    What it is
    The system narrating its decisions in real time with the actual numbers: why each signal fired or was held back, how stops and ladders were built, every management move, what each outcome taught it, and every desk action. This is how you verify the logic instead of trusting it.
